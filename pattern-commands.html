Pattern Commands

Commands can be registered as readable patterns instead of literal word lists. Literal words are matched phonetically (misheard words still match), while the special tokens give you configuration-style control over each trigger:

  • word matched phonetically
  • (word)? optional word or group
  • (word|other) required choice between alternatives
  • (word|other)? optional choice between alternatives
  • $name captures one word as params.name
  • *name captures all remaining words as params.name
